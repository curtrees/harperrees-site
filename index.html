Personal · Travel

I tell stories about people and the places that shape them.

My early years were shaped by the Driftless region of Wisconsin, a corner of the Midwest the glaciers never flattened. In La Crosse, the Mississippi winds past limestone bluffs, fog settles into the coulees at dawn, and the light over the river changes by the minute. When my family moved to Lexington, Kentucky, I found limestone under the bluegrass too, running beneath the horse farms and the old dry-stone fences of horse country. Both places taught me to slow down and pay attention to a landscape and to the people who make their lives in it. I bring that way of seeing to every frame.

Now I’m studying directing in the BFA Film and Television Production program at Chapman University’s Dodge College of Film and Media Arts. My work runs from short films to two-minute pieces made for social.

I also know the work behind the camera. As a Student Admin Assistant in the Dodge College dean’s office, I handle scheduling, project coordination and events, including the college’s Master Classes. It’s the planning that keeps a production on track.

I cut in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve, and I spent spring 2026 studying in Florence, Italy.
